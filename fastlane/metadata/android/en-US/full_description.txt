<p><i>AlternativeUnlockXposed</i> provides a reliable way to run commands when entering a specific, wrong PIN on your Android lock screen. Unlike <a href='https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=me.lucky.duress' target='_blank' rel='nofollow noopener'>Duress</a>, this app uses Xposed Framework so you can also unlock your phone with a wrong PIN, preventing some <i>social engineering vulnerabilities</i> 😇 And by the way, it also works before your first unlock after a reboot.</p><p><br><b>Features</b></p><ul><li>Alternative PIN to unlock phone</li><li>Run commands on alternative PIN with root</li><li>Easy to use user interface</li><li>Material You design</li></ul><p>Note that this is an Xposed app, so it may not work on older Android versions.</p>
